# Accessibility baseline — 2026-09-26

Code audit of the non-3D chrome on Trust Your Sign (closed beta). The 3D sky is unchanged. This pass makes the overlay operable from the keyboard and gives screen readers names, landmarks, and a way out of the intro.

Not a full WCAG audit and not a device screen-reader pass. Gaps below are still open.

## What was audited

## What shipped

### Skip paths

- The existing **Skip to content** link stays first in the tab order and above the intro veil. When the sign dock is on screen it moves focus to that dock (`#sky-chrome`). While the dock is still inert it moves focus to `#main-content`, so the next Tab reaches Skip.
- **Skip the introduction** is a real control during the “The Universe Asks You…” veil (the header row is not mounted then) and again in the sky header while the belt is still fading in. Activating it finishes the intro immediately — it does not wait out the 0.4s accidental-tap gate used for pointer noise — and moves focus to the sign dock.
- **Skip the entrance** (the dive into a sign) is labeled and, when the landing finishes, moves focus to Back.

### Focus order

- Sky, top to bottom: Skip to content, then either Skip intro or the header (support, Pause, Skip, Sign in), then the title, then house links, the twelve signs, Enter this sign, then “How a chart talks here.”
- The belt and the title gloss stay out of the tab order until the belt has faded in enough to use (`inert` while it is still a ghost). The fade itself still paints. The WebGL canvas is `aria-hidden` and removed from the tab order so it does not sit in front of the chrome.
- Arrow keys, Home, and End move along the belt in calendar order. The index they write is still the astrological sign index.
- Birth chat keeps glossary words after the form in the tab order, and the first field of the current step takes initial focus. Escape closes the dialog. Escape on an open month/day/year menu does not.
- Sign-in: header, then the account-mode pair (pressed state), then the named form. The “already signed in” view has the same `#main-content` target as every other page.

### Names and landmarks

- Landmarks: sky header (“Sky controls”), sign dock (“Choose a sign”), chart header (“Chart”), chart rooms (already “Chart rooms”), sign galaxy header, house links (“House”), login header.
- Pressed: Pause / Resume (already), each sign on the belt (already), chart rooms (already), Sign in / Create account.
- Expanded: support orb (already), chart sheet (already), glossary words (already). Account menu still uses the Radix trigger’s expanded state.
- Enter this sign and Begin birth chart include the sign name in the accessible name, and the visible label is still part of that name.

## Remaining gaps

- The live sky, planet picking, drag-to-fly, and pinch are still pointer and gesture. The belt and Enter this sign are the keyboard path across signs. There is no continuous keyboard cruise along the corridor.
- No live region announces the sign under the camera while a gesture fly is in motion. A polite live region there would talk over the whole trip.
- Inside a galaxy, look-around is still pointer or WASD / arrows once you are in the sky. Those arrows are not the belt. Star points are buttons; the 3D star field itself is not a screen-reader map.
- Chart rooms are buttons with a pressed state, not a tab widget (no arrow-key roving on the dock). Number keys 1–7 still switch rooms when a chart is open.
- Gloss words in the title are extra tab stops once the title is visible. That is intentional; they are not grouped into a single control.
- Contrast of type sitting on a bright sky was not measured in this pass. Closed-beta HUD contrast work is separate.
- Auth screens other than `/login` (account, reset password) were not re-walked control by control. They already use `#main-content`.
- This was a source audit. It was not confirmed with VoiceOver, NVDA, or a keyboard-only pass on a phone.

## Indexing

Closed beta stays closed. `public/robots.txt` is still `Disallow: /`. `public/sitemap.xml` lists the public house pages only, and the Sitemap line in `robots.txt` is commented until the product opens indexing.
